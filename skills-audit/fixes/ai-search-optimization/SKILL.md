# AI Search Optimization — get cited, not just ranked

Mikke's buyers increasingly ask an AI assistant, not Google: "how do I get
ready for a DHS 245G audit," "AI automation for a small therapy practice,"
"is there software for 254B compliance tracking." This skill makes Vibe
Align, SUDSync, and CADENCE the answer those assistants cite.

## The core shift

Google ranks pages; AI assistants extract and cite *answers*. Optimizing
for citation means writing content that is quotable, verifiable, and
unambiguous about who it's from and what it covers.

## Step 1 — Query map (do this before writing anything)

List the 5–10 questions a target buyer would actually ask an AI, in their
words (pull from customer-voice-research language banks when available).
Group them: compliance questions → SUDSync, automation questions → Vibe
Align consulting, intake/voice questions → CADENCE.

## Step 2 — Content rules for citability

1. **Answer first.** Every page/post leads with a direct 2–3 sentence
   answer to one specific question — the extractable block. Diagnosis and
   nuance come after, in Mikke's voice as always.
2. **One question per page.** "Everything about 245G" pages get skipped;
   "What documentation does a 245G program need for group notes?" gets cited.
3. **Claims carry receipts.** Statute numbers, dates, and named sources
   inline (245G subpart, effective date). AI systems prefer content they
   can verify; it also happens to be how Mikke builds trust anyway.
4. **Entity clarity.** Every page states plainly who Mikke is (LADC, CSAM,
   SAP; former state-contracted auditor) and what the product does, in
   text — not just in a logo. Consistent naming across site, LinkedIn,
   and directories so the entity graph connects.
5. **Freshness signals.** Date-stamp content; refresh high-value answers
   when rules change (pairs with compliance-check's knowledge of what
   changed in 245G).
6. **FAQ blocks + structured data.** FAQPage/Article/Organization schema
   on the site; Q&A-formatted sections that mirror the query map verbatim.

## Step 3 — Audit mode

Given a URL or draft, score it against the six rules above with specific
fixes, then check: does this page answer one mapped query better than
anything else that exists? If not, say what's missing or whether the page
should merge into another.

## Step 4 — Verify (quarterly)

Ask the major assistants the mapped queries (via crosscheck where set up)
and record whether Vibe Align/SUDSync appears, what IS cited, and what the
cited pages do differently. Feed findings back into the query map.
